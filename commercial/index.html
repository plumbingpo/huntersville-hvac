Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Restaurants and food-service businesses have climate demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Kitchens generate enormous heat loads while dining rooms must stay comfortable, exhaust hoods pull out conditioned air that makeup air units have to replace, and a busy Friday night pushes rooftop units to their limit. Huntersville HVAC specializes in food-service HVAC, including rooftop package units, split systems, correct tonnage and airflow sizing, makeup air balancing against hood exhaust, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

Staff can't work a hot line in a kitchen that's pulling negative pressure, and customers notice the drafts and the stuffy dining room right away. We keep temperatures and airflow where your operation needs them, then leave behind service logs and written reports after each visit. That paperwork demonstrates a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors and covers the documentation many commercial leases call for.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Professional Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, confirm correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and check ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is simple: protecting the people in your building from CO hazards, poor air quality, and water damage. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. In a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it leads to tenant disputes. Huntersville HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Huntersville and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, get the building comfortable again, and record every step for your service records. Then we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
